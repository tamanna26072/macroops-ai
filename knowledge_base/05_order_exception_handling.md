# Order Exception Handling

Document ID: SOP-005 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Customer Support Lead
Note: synthetic prototype document for MacroOps AI. Values are prototype assumptions.

## Late deliveries
- 1 to 10 minutes late: no compensation, the order is logged as a breach.
- More than 10 minutes late: the customer receives a credit of 10 percent of the order value, up to 50.
- More than 20 minutes late: the customer receives a credit of 20 percent of the order value and the store manager reviews the order.

## Cancelled orders
Orders can be cancelled by the customer before picking starts at no cost. After picking starts, the store may charge a handling fee. Cancellations caused by long queue time or missing items are tagged as store-caused and counted against the store.

## Failed deliveries
A delivery fails when the customer is unreachable, the address is wrong or the order cannot be fulfilled. The rider must call the customer twice and wait 5 minutes before marking the order failed. Failed orders with perishable items are written off and the reason is recorded.

## Refunds
Refunds for missing or damaged items are issued automatically within 24 hours. Customers who report a missing item more than 3 times in a month are reviewed by support.

## Reporting
Customer support sends a daily summary of cancellations, failures and compensation to the area managers. A store with a cancellation rate above 4 percent for a week must submit a root cause analysis.
