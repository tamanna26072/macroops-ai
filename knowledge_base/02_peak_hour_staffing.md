# Peak Hour Staffing

Document ID: SOP-002 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Area Manager
Note: synthetic prototype document for MacroOps AI. Values are prototype assumptions.

## Peak windows
Peak hours are 8 to 10 in the morning, 12 to 14 at lunch, and 18 to 22 in the evening. The evening window is the busiest, with breach rates about three times higher than off-peak hours.

## Staffing rules
- Every store must have its full roster present 30 minutes before a peak window starts.
- A store should have at least one picker for every 8 orders expected per hour. Stores with fewer than 40 staff on a shift must request support from a nearby store before the evening peak.
- Breaks are not scheduled during peak windows except for mandatory rest.
- Supervisors stay on the floor during peaks and do not do back-office work.

## Understaffed stores
A store is understaffed when the pending task ratio is above 0.18 or when more than 5 orders per 100 staff arrived in the last 60 minutes. The store manager must do the following:
1. Call the area manager for support staff.
2. Pause low-priority tasks such as shelf restocking and returns.
3. Ask the dispatcher to stop accepting scheduled orders for the next hour if the queue exceeds 10 orders.

## Event days
On promotion days and during heavy rain, the area manager adds 20 percent extra pickers and riders for the full day. Order volume can rise by 50 percent on promotion days.

## Review
The area manager reviews headcount against order volume every week and adjusts rosters for stores that breach more than 35 percent of orders.
