# SLA Policy

Document ID: SOP-001 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Operations Head
Note: synthetic prototype document for MacroOps AI. Values are prototype assumptions.

## Purpose
This policy defines how delivery promises are made, measured and escalated across all dark stores.

## Definitions
- Promised time: the delivery time shown to the customer at checkout. It is based on basket size and delivery distance.
- SLA breach: an order delivered after its promised time.
- Severe breach: an order delivered more than 10 minutes after its promised time.
- On-time delivery (OTD): the share of delivered orders that were not breached.

## Targets
- Network OTD target is 90 percent of delivered orders per day.
- Severe breach rate must stay below 3 percent per day.
- Cancellation rate must stay below 2 percent and failed deliveries below 0.5 percent.
- Cancelled and failed orders are not counted in OTD. They are tracked as separate KPIs.

## Escalation levels
- Level 1, store alert: a store falls below 85 percent OTD in any rolling two-hour window. The store manager must act within 15 minutes.
- Level 2, area alert: a store stays below 80 percent OTD for a full day. The area manager reviews staffing and stock and files a corrective action plan within 24 hours.
- Level 3, network alert: network OTD falls below 80 percent for a day. The operations head convenes a review and decides on temporary measures such as extending promised times or pausing promotions.

## Promised time changes
Promised times may be extended temporarily during heavy rain, major promotions or rider shortages. Any extension must be approved by the area manager and logged.

## AI risk alerts
The SLA risk model flags orders likely to breach. A HIGH alert must be reviewed by the store dispatcher within 5 minutes. See the rider assignment and picker guidelines for the actions to take.
