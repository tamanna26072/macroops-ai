# Incident Playbooks

Document ID: SOP-009 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Area Manager
Note: synthetic prototype document for MacroOps AI. Values are prototype assumptions.

## What to do during heavy rain
Rider speed drops by about a third and assignment takes longer. The dispatch lead adds extra riders and moves riders from low-volume stores. Promised times are extended by 5 minutes through the app. Orders over 6 km are paused unless a rider with a good record accepts. The area manager is told at the start of the rain and logs the extension.

## How to handle a promotion day surge
Order volume can rise by 50 percent. The area manager adds 20 percent extra pickers and riders for the full day. Fast moving SKUs are restocked before the first peak. Stores stop accepting scheduled orders when the picking queue is above 10 orders. The inventory lead watches stockouts every two hours.

## What to do when there is a rider shortage
If more than 20 percent of packed orders wait for a rider, the dispatch lead moves riders from nearby stores and offers a small incentive for the next two hours. If waiting continues for 30 minutes, the area manager extends promised times and informs customer support.

## What to do when the app or order system is down
1. The store manager informs the area manager within 10 minutes.
2. Switch to manual dispatch. Orders are noted on paper and sent to riders by phone call or the rider group chat.
3. Customer support sends a delay message to affected customers and extends promised times.
4. After recovery, the dispatcher enters all manual orders so that records and refunds are correct.
5. The area manager files an incident report within 24 hours.

## What to do when a popular item runs out in many stores
If a SKU is out of stock in more than 3 stores of one area, the inventory lead raises an urgent replenishment request and approved substitutes are switched on in the app. Customers are told before dispatch when a major item is missing.

## After any incident
The area manager checks the daily OTD and breach trend, records the cause and confirms that rosters and stock levels are back to normal.
