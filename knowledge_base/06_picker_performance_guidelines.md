# Picker Performance Guidelines

Document ID: SOP-006 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Store Operations Lead
Note: synthetic prototype document for MacroOps AI. Values are prototype assumptions.

## Picking time standards
- The standard pick time is about 1.5 minutes plus 1 minute per item. A typical order of 4 to 5 items should be picked in 8 minutes.
- Picking more than 2.5 minutes per item is slow and is flagged to the supervisor.
- Any single order that takes more than 15 minutes to pick must be checked by the supervisor immediately.

## Picker scorecard
Each picker is measured monthly on minutes per item, missing item rate and the breach rate of the orders they picked. Pickers with at least 30 orders are included. The supervisor reviews the slowest 10 percent each month.

## Coaching
1. Review three slow orders with the picker to find the cause, such as poor shelf knowledge or a long walking path.
2. Share the optimised pick route and the shelf layout.
3. Re-measure after two weeks. Pickers who do not improve are moved to packing tasks or retrained.

## AI risk alerts at the picking stage
When the AI model flags an order as HIGH risk after picking, the supervisor checks whether packing can be finished faster and asks the dispatcher to prioritise rider assignment for that order. Orders flagged at the order stage should be picked first, ahead of orders with a later promised time.

## Queue management
If the queue at the picking stage exceeds 10 orders, the supervisor assigns extra pickers from packing and stops accepting scheduled orders for the next hour.
