# Stockout and Missing Item Handling

Document ID: SOP-003 | Version 1.0 | Owner: Inventory Lead
Note: synthetic prototype document for MacroOps AI. Values are prototype assumptions.

## Why it matters
Missing items slow down picking. Each missing item adds about two minutes while the picker searches or substitutes. About one in five orders has at least one missing item, and stores with high stockout rates breach more often.

## Phantom stock
Phantom stock is when the system shows stock but the shelf is empty. The picker must report it through the app immediately so the SKU is blocked from further orders.

## Picker procedure for a missing item
1. Check the neighbouring shelf and the back storage once, for no more than 60 seconds.
2. If the item is not found, mark it missing in the app. Do not keep searching.
3. Offer the approved substitute shown in the app. If no substitute exists, remove the item.
4. If more than 2 items are missing or the missing value is over 30 percent of the basket, call the customer before dispatch.

## Cycle counts
- Stores with inventory accuracy below 90 percent must run a full cycle count every week.
- Stores with a stockout rate above 8 percent must count their top 100 selling SKUs every day before opening.
- Any SKU with a stock variance larger than 10 units must be recounted within 24 hours.

## Replenishment
The inventory lead raises an urgent replenishment request when a SKU is out of stock in more than 3 stores of the same area. Fast moving SKUs are replenished before the morning and evening peaks.
